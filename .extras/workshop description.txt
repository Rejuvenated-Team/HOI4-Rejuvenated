[h1]Rejuvenated[/h1]
Rejuvenated is a large-scale overhaul/content mod. It aims to overhaul a large amount of pre-existing game content, as well add a great deal to it.

[h1]New Content[/h1]
[img]https://i.imgur.com/577f0NE.png[/img]
[i]Countries with a New Focus Tree[/i]
[list]
[*][b]Generic Focus Tree[/b] - A brand new generic focus tree. A good bit more interesting than the base game's!
[*][b]New States[/b] - All across the world, from the Americas & Madagascar, to Indonesia, Malaysia, & New Zealand.
[*][b]Resource Updates[/b] - Many countries now have more realistic & functional resources.
[*][b]Base Mechanic Changes[/b] - Conditional peace deals, agency improvements, smaller airwings, more dockyards per ship, reduced energy demands, faster wargoal justifications, and more!
[*][b]Global Population Overhaul[/b] - Tired of a decent chunk of world population being missing? Thanos couldn't keep everyone away forever, & they've been restored!
[*][b]New Formables[/b]
[*][b]Tech Tree Adjustments[/b]
[*][b]Unit Adjustments[/b] - Armored Cars have been made far more affordable, & much better at scouting & a few terrains.
[*][b]Misc. Country Tweaks[/b]
[*][b]Building Updates[/b] - Electrical grids are no longer mutually exclusive, & no longer use a shared building slot with factories.
[*][b]New Division Icons[/b]
[/list]

[h1]Information[/h1]

New update releases on the 20th of each month.

If you'd like to either contribute to this project or you're simply interested in getting the latest on updates & progress, check out our [url=https://discord.gg/NPa6fs7tSV]Discord Server[/url]!

If you want to experience our latest changes, check out our [url=https://github.com/Rejuvenated-Team/HOI4-Rejuvenated]GitHub[/url].
